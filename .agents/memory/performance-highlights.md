---
name: Performance highlights
description: How ad-set, creative, and repeat-customer rankings stay grounded in synced data
---

Performance highlights are ranked from persisted ad-set, creative, and customer/order metrics for the selected date range. Suggestions should include the winning entity and supporting evidence such as spend, revenue, ROAS, CTR, conversions, order count, or average order value.

**Why:** Campaign-level metrics cannot truthfully identify the best ad set or creative. Connectors that do not expose those granular entities must remain empty rather than displaying inferred or placeholder winners.

**How to apply:** Add new ad platforms by mapping their native ad-group/ad or ad-set/ad objects into the normalized entity tables and daily metric tables. Keep demo backfills explicitly demo-only and preserve empty-state messaging until a connector sync supplies entity-level data.
